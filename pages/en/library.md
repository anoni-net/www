This section collects long-form external documents that the community has translated into Traditional Chinese, including research reports and guidance from standards bodies. We pick documents on internet censorship, surveillance or anonymity networks that matter locally to Traditional Chinese readers, for example because a report names a Taiwanese company or analyzes censorship technology from a neighboring country. Each translation comes with a link to the original, translation notes and an editorial statement, plus translator's notes where needed.

!!! info "Translations are in Traditional Chinese only"

    Each English page below is an overview of the document, with links to the original and to our Traditional Chinese (zh-TW) translation. If you read English, the original is the primary source. A separate English version of these translations is not maintained.

Single-piece explainers and guides to upstream research are in [Articles](/articles/), and the community's own measurement figures are in the [Quarterly Reports](/projects/reports/).
