Published by InterSecLab in September 2025, the report analyzes roughly 500 GB of leaked files to show how Geedge Networks (积至公司 / 积至（海南）信息技术有限公司) turned Great Firewall technology into a product line, sold it to the governments of Kazakhstan, Ethiopia, Pakistan and Myanmar, and deployed it in Xinjiang and other Chinese provinces. It covers deep packet inspection, real-time monitoring of mobile subscribers, censorship rules tailored to each region, and the techniques used to block Tor, VPNs and Psiphon.

* Original report: [The Internet Coup: A Technical Analysis on How a Chinese Company is Exporting The Great Firewall to Autocratic Regimes](https://interseclab.org/research/the-internet-coup/)
* Traditional Chinese translation: [網路政變 (zh-TW)](zh-tw:/library/interseclab-network-coup/)

!!! info "Translated into Traditional Chinese only"
