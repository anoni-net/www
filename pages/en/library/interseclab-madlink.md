MADLink, published in April 2026, is the first in InterSecLab's supply-chain series following The Internet Coup. It documents how Taiwan-listed ADLINK Technologies (`6166`) shipped 1,708 CSA-7400 high-density network appliances to Geedge Networks between 2019 and 2020. The hardware was deployed in Kazakhstan to run Geedge's flagship Tiangou Secure Gateway (TSG), and ADLINK components were also found in the EtherFabric network packet brokers deployed in Myanmar. The report includes ADLINK's response, a written statement from Taiwan's International Trade Administration, and legislator Puma Shen's criticism of Taiwan's export control framework.
