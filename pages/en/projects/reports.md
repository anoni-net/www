The [Tor Relay Watch](/projects/pulse/) and [OONI Coverage](/projects/asn-coverage/) pages show the last 60 days. The quarterly reports fix each quarter's numbers in place, compare the start and end of the quarter and the quarter before, and add the community's reading of the numbers. The raw numbers for each report are on [GitHub](https://github.com/anoni-net/www/tree/main/data/reports). The reports are currently published in Traditional Chinese only.
