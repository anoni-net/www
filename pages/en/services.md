This software is developed by other open-source projects, and the community sets it up, updates it and keeps it running. We run it ourselves to depend less on third parties, keep control of our data, and give community discussion and sensitive collaboration a foundation we can vouch for. The content and code the community writes itself are listed under [projects](/projects/).

Matrix, CryptPad and the Formbricks backend need an account issued by a person, so write to <whisper@anoni.net>. The community works asynchronously, so expect a reply within a day or two.

## Joining Matrix

1. Use [Element](https://matrix.anoni.net/) on the web, or [download Element X](https://element.io/download) and set the homeserver to `im.anoni.net`. An existing `matrix.org` account works fine.
2. Once registered, open the community's [public Space](https://matrix.to/#/#community:im.anoni.net) to join the community rooms in one go, or join individual rooms as you need them.

For why we self-host Matrix and the privacy trade-offs behind it, see [From Discord’s Age Verification to Why We Self-Host Matrix](docs:blog/2026/02/2026-discord-matrix-statement/).

## Good to know

- **CryptPad** has shipped Traditional and Simplified Chinese as built-in locales since 2026.5.0, switchable from the settings page. The story of the two and a half years of upstream translation behind that is in [this announcement](docs:blog/2026/05/2026-cryptpad-zh-hant/).
- **Etherpad** works as a throwaway chat when you meet someone in person and neither of you wants to swap app accounts. Open a new pad, hand over the URL and use the chat sidebar, then clear the pad when you are done. Nothing is encrypted, so the server can see it.
- **Send** gives the server no access to file contents and keeps no routine access logs. What it stores and for how long is in [How send.anoni.net handles your data](docs:community/send-privacy/).

## Video calls (external)

Online meetings use [Jitsi](https://jitsi.goodmeet.asia/), offered free by a third party rather than run by the community, so its terms and availability are theirs. Meeting links are posted in the relevant Matrix room.
